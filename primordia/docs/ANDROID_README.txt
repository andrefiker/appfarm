PRIMORDIA 1.1.0 — ANDROID

Install Primordia-1.1.0-Android.apk on your Android phone. When prompted,
allow installation from the app you used to open this APK. Launch Primordia.
The game runs offline in landscape with no account or extra downloads.
ARM64 phones with Android 5.0+ and OpenGL ES 3.0 are supported by this build.
Modern ARM64 phones are the intended target; older hardware is untested.

SWIM: drag the large circular pad on the right. Your organism faces that way.
BURST / BITE: tap the large ability button with another finger while swimming.
ESCAPE / ABILITY: tap the smaller ability button. Anatomy changes its effect.
EVOLVE: gather at least 18 mutation and 60 energy, then tap Evolve.

EDITOR: choose a part, tap beside the membrane to place it, then drag its
attachment. Rotate, resize and remove the selected part with the buttons.
PAIRS toggles mirrored placement of new parts. BODY changes proportions.
Pigment and pattern are free. The three choices below the organism grant a
2-point discount on a newly added structure of the chosen kind.
REPRODUCE commits a descendant. CANCEL keeps your current organism.

Pause opens the map, lineage and settings. Settings can mirror the controls
for right-handed play and mute sound. Android Back pauses/resumes or returns
from a menu. Putting the app in the background pauses and saves progress.
Continue restores your lineage after closing and reopening the app.

Saves stay in the app's private local storage. There is no Internet permission,
telemetry, login, advertising, cloud service, camera or microphone access.
The game keeps a last-good save backup. Uninstalling or clearing app data
removes local progress. Installing a future APK with the same package and
signing key preserves app data; the source build must reuse that key.

See README.md and docs/ANDROID_RELEASE.md in the source for build and test
information. Package: com.andrefiker.primordia. Version code: 1.
