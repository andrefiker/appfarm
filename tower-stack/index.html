How to play & scoring

Drag anywhere in the building area to aim. Let go to drop, or use the wide DROP button. Wait for the impact to settle. ↕ shows the full tower. Rotate is optional; both hands have equal access.

A scored block earns 100 + 10 per whole metre + 25 if stable + 5 per consecutive stable placement (up to 50) + alignment bonus (0–30, or 0–100 in Precision). Each block scores once.

One standard block is 1 m tall. Missing the platform ends a competitive run. After 3 placements, losing 40% of scored blocks (minimum 2), or remaining below 38% of peak height for 1.5 s, ends the run. Minor swaying is allowed. Zen and Sandbox never end on collapse. Time Attack stops at exactly 3 minutes of active play; only settled placements count.

Continue replays saved drops and simulation ticks, including falling blocks. It may take a moment for long sessions. Pausing or backgrounding freezes the timer. No network, ads, or data collection.
